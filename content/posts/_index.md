I write to understand. Turning a half-formed idea into a post forces me to find the parts I don't actually understand yet.

Two beliefs drive this blog. Knowledge shouldn't hide behind jargon: when people understand how things work, they can build on it. And most complexity is just unfamiliarity. A language seems hard until you know its symbols and rules. Strip away the jargon and the idea underneath is usually simple.

Posts are grouped into series. Each series builds from the basics, so it's best read in order.
